Local Processing
Your recording is processed locally by this application. The app is designed to avoid sending audio to external services. All neural inference and feature extraction execute directly on your machine.
⚠️
Important Limitations
This tool provides an automated screening result, not definitive proof of authenticity. Noise, compression, recording conditions, and unfamiliar speech-generation methods may affect the result. The candidate threshold (0.4820) is unvalidated for general real-world use.
🔒 Local Privacy Guarantee: All audio decoding and neural network inference execute locally on your machine. No waveforms, audio files, or metadata are transmitted to external servers or cloud services.

⚠️ Limitations & Ethical Use: This application is a machine-learning screening tool designed for research evaluation. The V7 model was trained on the ASVspoof 2019 dataset. Acoustic variations caused by strong compression (e.g. messaging apps), background noise, heavy reverberation, or microphone anomalies may affect scores. Its output should not be treated as definitive legal proof of authenticity or synthetic fabrication.
